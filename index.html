Meng Wang

Hi 👋, I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at Hunan University. I received my Ph.D. from the College of Computer Science and Electronic Engineering, Hunan University, in 2025.

My research interests include autonomous driving perception, 3D scene perception, semantic scene completion, and robotics. I have served as a reviewer for journals and conferences including TCSVT, TII, TMM, ICLR, AAAI, NeurIPS, ICML, and CVPR.
